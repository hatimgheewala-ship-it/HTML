Exploring the Solar System

Our Amazing Planets

The solar system is a huge collection of planets, moons, asteroids, and other objects. The Sun is at the centre and provides light and heat to the planets.

Learn more about our solar system here: NASA Solar System Guide

The Red Planet

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun. Scientists study Mars to learn more about its surface, atmosphere, and whether it may have once had conditions suitable for life.

Explore more information about Mars here: NASA Mars Guide

Interesting Space Facts

Stars Are Giant

Stars are enormous balls of hot gas that produce their own light and heat. Our Sun is also a star and is much closer to Earth than other stars.

The Moon Has No Light of Its Own

The Moon does not create its own light. It looks bright because it reflects light from the Sun.

Read more about the Moon here: NASA Moon Guide

Quick Space Note

Did you know? Light from the Sun takes about eight minutes to travel from the Sun to Earth.
