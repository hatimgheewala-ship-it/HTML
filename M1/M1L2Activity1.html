Welcome to the amazing world of space. Let's discover some interesting facts about our universe!

🌙 Fact 1: The Moon

The Moon is Earth's natural satellite. It takes about 27 days to complete one orbit around Earth.

Why does the Moon shine?

The Moon does not produce its own light. It reflects sunlight, which makes it appear bright in the night sky.

☀️ Fact 2: The Sun

The Sun is a star located at the centre of our solar system. It provides Earth with light and heat.

How hot is the Sun?

The surface of the Sun has a temperature of about 5,500 degrees Celsius.

How far is the Sun?

The Sun is approximately 150 million kilometres away from Earth.

🪐 Fact 3: The Planets

Our solar system has eight planets. They travel around the Sun in different orbits.

Which planet is the largest?

Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system. It is a gas giant with many moons.

Fun comparison

More than 1,000 Earths could fit inside Jupiter by volume.
