🌳 Why Plants Are Important

Plants are like the superheroes of our planet — they give us oxygen, food, shade, and help keep the Earth clean and beautiful.

Trees, flowers, grass, and vegetables are all part of nature. Taking care of plants helps create a healthier world for everyone!

🌿 Quick Nature Tips

  • 🌱 Plant a tree or flower
  • 💧 Save water whenever possible
  • ♻ Recycle paper, plastic, and glass
  • 🐾 Be kind to animals
